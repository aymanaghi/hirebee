Privacy Policy

Last updated

This policy explains what HireBee collects, why, and how you control it. HireBee is operated by in Lebanon.

What we collect

  • Telegram details: your Telegram user ID, name and username, which Telegram shares with every bot you use.
  • Your CV: the CV you upload or build (for example work history, skills, education, contact details).
  • Your activity: jobs matched to you, CVs you tailored, applications you track, and companies you follow.
  • Payments: plan, amount, date, payment method and transaction reference. For Whish, the receipt screenshot you send. We never see or store card details, wallet recovery phrases or private keys.

How we use it

  • To create your CVs and cover letters, find and score jobs for you, and run your tracker.
  • To turn on your plan when you pay and to send you reminders you've asked for.
  • To keep HireBee working and prevent abuse.

We don't sell your data, and we don't use it for advertising.

Who we share it with

  • Anthropic (Claude AI): your CV and the job text are sent to create your tailored documents and job scores.
  • Telegram: messages and files are delivered through Telegram, and Stars payments are processed by Telegram.
  • Whish Money and the TON network, when you choose those payment methods.

Each provider handles data under its own privacy policy. We only send what's needed for the task.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while you use HireBee. Send /delete to the bot to erase your CV, matches, applications and followed companies at any time. We keep a minimal record of payments (amount, date, reference) for accounting.

Your rights

You can see your data in the app, download your CV, and delete everything with /delete. For any other request, email .

Changes

If we change this policy in an important way, we'll tell you in the bot.
