    Questions

    Can't find your answer? Email us.

    Does HireBee apply to jobs for me?

    No. HireBee finds jobs and prepares your CV and cover letter; you choose where to apply and send them yourself. Nothing is ever sent in your name.

    Will it make things up on my CV?

    No. Your tailored CV only uses experience and skills you already have; HireBee reorders and rewrites them to fit the job. It also shows you which skills a job asks for that you don't list.

    Which jobs can I find?

    Remote jobs from RemoteOK, Remotive, Himalayas, We Work Remotely and Jobicy that are open to people in Lebanon; NGO and UN jobs in Lebanon from ReliefWeb; jobs in Lebanon from company careers pages and from Lebanese job channels on Telegram that agreed to share their posts; and, with Ultra Max, new jobs from the career pages of companies you follow. HireBee doesn't copy jobs from LinkedIn or Bayt.

    How do I pay with Whish?

    Open your plan in the bot, choose "Pay on the website", and pay the exact amount with Whish. Then send the receipt screenshot to the bot; your plan turns on as soon as it's checked, usually within a few hours.

    How do I pay with USDT?

    On the payment page, tap "Open in Keeper": the amount, address and your order code are filled in. Your plan turns on automatically within a few minutes of the payment.

    Does it renew automatically?

    No. Each payment gives you 30 days. HireBee reminds you 3 days before the end, and you decide whether to pay again.

    Is HireBee in Arabic?

    Not yet: the app and the CVs are in English, which most employers hiring from Lebanon ask for.

    What happens to my CV?

    It's used only to make your documents and find matching jobs. It's never sold. You can delete everything at any time with /delete. Read the privacy policy.
