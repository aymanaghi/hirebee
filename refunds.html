Refund Policy

Last updated

We want HireBee to be worth what you pay. If it isn't, here's how refunds work.

Full refund within 7 days

If you're not happy, contact us within 7 days of paying and you'll get a full refund, as long as you've tailored 5 CVs or fewer since that payment.

If something went wrong

  • You paid twice, or paid but your plan didn't turn on: we fix it or refund you in full, any time.
  • HireBee was unavailable for more than 3 days in a row during your plan: we add the lost days to your plan, or refund them if you prefer.

How refunds are paid

  • Telegram Stars: refunded to your Stars balance through Telegram.
  • Whish: sent back to the Whish account that paid.
  • USDT on TON: sent back to the wallet that paid, minus the network fee.

Refunds are usually processed within 5 working days.

No automatic renewals

Plans never renew by themselves, so you're never charged without paying yourself.

Ask for a refund

Email or message with your order code or receipt.
